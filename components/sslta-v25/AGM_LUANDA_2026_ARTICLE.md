# South Sudan Participates in Tennis Africa Annual General Meeting in Luanda

**Luanda, Angola | 6–7 November 2026**

The Secretary General of the South Sudan Tennis Federation, Mr. Henrbole Festo Kenyi, participated in the Tennis Africa Annual General Meeting (AGM), held in Luanda, Angola, on 6–7 November 2026.

This participation follows the official acceptance of the South Sudan Tennis Federation as the 52nd full member of the Confederation of African Tennis (CAT), effective 1 January 2026.

The AGM coincided with the historic 2026 Angola Ladies Open, a WTA 125 tournament taking place for the first time on African soil at the Kikuxi Tennis Academy in Luanda, from 2–8 November 2026.

## South Sudan's Vision for Development

During the meeting, the Secretary General presented the South Sudan Tennis Federation's vision for developing the sport and discussed opportunities to support and promote tennis activities in the country.

On the sidelines of the AGM, Mr. Kenyi held an important meeting with Zone IV – East Africa Zone, to which South Sudan belongs.

The discussions addressed the country's urgent development needs, including:

- Coaches' training and certification
- Umpires' and referees' training
- Equipment and facilities support
- Junior development programmes

Zone IV comprises Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, Sudan and South Sudan.

## Chairmanship of the Meeting

The AGM was chaired by Mr. Jean-Claude Talon, President of Tennis Africa.

The meeting also witnessed the election of Mr. Isaac Aboagye Duah of Ghana as President of West Africa Zone and Vice President of Tennis Africa.

South Sudan is proud to raise its national flag high through its participation in continental tennis governance and development.
